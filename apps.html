Apps and Projects

A mix of apps and projects, some from school and some built for myself. The apps run in a web browser, so there is nothing to download. Each title opens the work itself. Where there is more to say, there is a More button for it.

Used Car Price Prediction Model

March 2025 · R, RStudio

A linear regression model trained on more than 100,000 Craigslist listings to predict used car prices. It landed on an R² of 0.691 and an average error of about $3,781. I checked it against real listings on Autotrader, and it came within 11 to 13 percent on common cars.

Carbon Cycle and Sustainable Deforestation Model

January 2025 · R, RStudio

A dynamic systems model looking at how deforestation changes carbon storage in an ecosystem, with seasonal climate shifts built in. I presented what I found to a full lecture hall.

DSA Cryptosystem

December 2024 · Python

A hybrid cryptosystem built on the Chinese Remainder Theorem and RSA. Then I broke it, using the exposed public key to show where the whole thing falls apart.

Drop in the data export Spotify emails you and get a dashboard of your listening. Your totals, a listening personality, your top artists and tracks, and a deep end of 101 odd stats. It all runs in your browser, so nothing is uploaded.

Ontario Fishing Regulations

Web app · Works offline

An interactive map of Ontario's 20 fishing zones. Tap your zone, or use your location, and see the seasons, catch limits, and what is open right now. It all comes from the official regulations, which otherwise sit in a 148-page PDF.

Site Journal

Web app · Works offline

A private journal for Ontario Parks campsites. Walk a campground, rate the sites, and remember which ones are worth booking next time. It covers every reservable park, down to the individual sites and trails.
