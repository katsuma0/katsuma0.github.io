Mac Remote

App · November 2026

GitHub ↗

For my contribution to this world slowly becoming the dystopian WALL-E movie, I created a remote controller for my Mac so I don't need to get up from my couch.

Why I made it

MacBook is across the room, I'm on the couch. The couch wins. Getting up to skip an ad on YouTube is a lot to ask...

What it does

  • Turns your iPhone into a remote controller for your MacBook.
  • Trackpad, typing, shortcuts, volume and media easily accessible to the most couchiest of potatoes out there.
