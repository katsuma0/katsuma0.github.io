ON site

App · July 2026

Open the web app ↗ Get it on the App Store ↗

A private journal for Ontario park campgrounds. For the low, low price of a nice walk around the campground, you can rate each site out of five, add a note or a photo, and mark the ones worth booking again.

Why I made it

Ever since I was young, my parents took me camping often. Lately I have been sharing that love with my friends and showing them the beauty of being outdoors. With that comes the responsibility of booking the campsites, and choosing a site you have never seen can be a gamble.

What it does

  • Covers Ontario's provincial parks: 124 parks, 372 campgrounds and about 20,000 individual sites, including backcountry sites at parks like Killarney, French River and The Massasauga, plus 211 trails. Algonquin's interior is not in it yet.
  • Rate each site out of five, add a note or a photo, and mark the ones worth booking again.
  • Search across parks, sites, trails, or even a fish, to find the parks that hold it. On iPhone and iPad, any park is one search away from the Home Screen.
  • Favourites, per-park stats, and a journal with filters and sorting for deciding what to book.
  • Safety and leave-no-trace notes under More.
  • Works with no signal: the iPhone and iPad app from the first launch, the web version once it has loaded. In English and French, light or dark.

What's next

I would like to work with Ontario Parks to implement:

  • Offline campground maps to navigate the park easier.
  • Site availability and reservations.
  • Fill out missing sites.

Not affiliated with Ontario Parks or the Government of Ontario. Book through their official site. Everything you save stays on your device; the details are in the privacy policy.
