ON site

App · July 2026 · Claude Code

Open the app ↗

A private journal for Ontario's provincial park campsites. I book the camping trips for my friends and family, and picking a site you have never seen is a gamble. Some are good and some are not, and a year later you cannot remember which was which. This is the notebook I wanted for that.

What it does

  • Covers Ontario's provincial parks: 124 parks, 372 campgrounds and about 20,000 individual sites, including backcountry sites at parks like Killarney, French River and The Massasauga, plus 211 trails. Algonquin's interior is not in it yet.
  • Rate each site out of five, add a note or a photo, and mark the ones worth booking again.
  • Search across parks, sites, trails, or even a fish, to find the parks that hold it. On iPhone and iPad, any park is one search away from the Home Screen.
  • Favourites, per-park stats, and a journal with filters and sorting for deciding what to book.
  • Safety and leave-no-trace notes under More.
  • Works with no signal: the iPhone and iPad app from the first launch, the web version once it has loaded. In English and French, light or dark.

Planned features

  • Offline campground maps
  • Missing backcountry sites
  • Public accounts
  • Site availability and reserving
  • Your feedback

Privacy

Everything you save stays on your device. There is no account, no server, and nothing is tracked. In the iPhone and iPad app your journal lives inside the app; on the web it lives in your browser's storage. You can export a backup to a file and load it on another device. The details are in the privacy policy. One ask from the app itself: please do not photograph occupied sites, and leave a site the way you would want to find it.

Not affiliated with Ontario Parks or the Government of Ontario. Book through their official site.

Part of a set

This is one of three field guides for Ontario that share the same look. The other two are On-Wildlife and On-Fishing, both on the projects page.
