Open Shutter

App · October 2026

GitHub ↗

A camera with every manual control iOS allows. Night shots and long exposures up to 30 minutes (Optimized for iPhone 18 Pro).

Why I made it

Apple locks the shutter speed of the iPhone 18 Pro to a maximum of only 1 second and the night mode on all iPhones to 10 seconds (or 30 seconds but very finicky). To capture the night sky properly night mode needs more time and precision. Open Shutter allows this.

What it does

  • Manual shutter, ISO, white balance and focus.
  • Long exposures up to 30 minutes, stacking 1-second frames.
  • Night mode that allows up to 30 minutes, not Apple's presets of 10 seconds.
