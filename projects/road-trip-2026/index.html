Road Trip 2026

Project · October 2026 · Python

Open the map ↗

A map of my drive from Markham to British Columbia and back, September 5 to October 8, 2026. The line comes from the track my inReach logged every ten minutes along the way.

The trip in numbers

  • 11,127 km over 34 days, about 191 hours of it moving.
  • The highest point was 1,660 m. The farthest point was 3,362 km from home.
  • A table of every day, with distance, moving time, and climbing.

How it is made

A small Python script reads the track and works out the numbers, then writes one page with a Leaflet map. It ignores the little jumps a parked device makes, smooths the elevation before counting the climbing, and breaks the line wherever the device went quiet for more than six hours, so a gap never gets counted as driving. There are four of those, the longest about 16 hours.

Privacy

The trip starts and ends at home, so every point within a kilometre and a half of the start is cut before the page is built.
