Same Spot

App · November 2026

App Store ↗ GitHub ↗

What's the point of raking the leaves without a satisfying before and after photo? Or ever wanted to create a long timelapse of the seasons changing in your backyard?

What it does

  • Create long timelapses, or before and after photos.
  • With an original used as a transparent overlay, you can line it up and shoot from the same spot.
