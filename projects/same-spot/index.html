Same Spot

App · October 2026 · Claude Code

View on GitHub ↗

An iPhone camera for timelapses that take months. Pick a spot and take one photo. Every time you come back, that photo shows up as a faint ghost over the camera, so you can line it up and shoot from the same place. After enough days, it turns the photos into a video.

What it does

  • Each project is one spot. The first photo is the guide and also day one of the video.
  • The guide sits over the live camera at 35% strength, with a slider to change it. Hold a button to see it at full strength.
  • When a scene changes too much to line up with the original, like after snow or once the leaves are gone, switch the guide to your last photo instead.
  • Reminders on the days and times you pick, such as morning and night every day. Tapping one opens the camera on that project.
  • Every shot uses the same lens and the same orientation as the first, so a landscape project stays landscape.
  • Make Timelapse turns the photos into a video at 6 to 30 photos per second, in a standard size that plays anywhere or a sharper high-resolution version. Share it or save it to Photos.

Privacy

Photos are saved in the app's own folder on your phone, not your camera roll, and nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Worth knowing

There is no iCloud sync yet, and no way to import old photos from your camera roll. It is written in Swift and the source is on GitHub.
