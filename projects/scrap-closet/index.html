Scrap Closet

App · November 2026

App Store ↗ Open the web app ↗ GitHub ↗

Ever thrown together an outfit you thought of... at 3am? Just me? Instead of raiding thru my own closet like a gremlin, I fit my closet into my phone.

Why I made it

I've always wanted to have an easily accessible wardrobe at my fingertips that doesn't leave a disregarded pile of clothes when trying to put together an outfit.

What it does

  • Removes the background from your clothes and accessories automatically.
  • Sort your closet, just to realize you own one too many green things.
  • Put your clothes together into outfits.
  • Track what you've worn.

What's next

  • Weather-based suggestions for Ontario's ever-changing seasons.
  • Accounts, so you can see other people's closets.
