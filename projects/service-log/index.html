Service Log

App · November 2026

GitHub ↗

A log for everything you do to your car. Oil, tires, brakes, filters, so next time you're at the shop you know exactly what you used and what you paid.

Why I made it

Before I made the app, I kept track of the work done on my car with pen and paper. Between services the paper would get lost, and I ended up getting a second alignment only a couple of months after the first.

What it does

  • A quick report for each job: service, date, odometer, price, shop and notes.
  • Keeps track of more than one vehicle.
