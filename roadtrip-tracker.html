Road trip tracker

Tool · records on this phone, publishes nothing by itself

This page turns the phone into the trip recorder. Press start when you set off, leave the page open on the dash, and press stop when you park for the night. It keeps a GPS point roughly every hundred metres, saved on this phone only, until you export a day and push it to the site.

publishing a day

  • Fill in where you stayed, then export the day's JSON.
  • Get the file to the laptop (airdrop, email to yourself, whatever works).
  • Drop it in trip/days/ in the site repo, exactly as downloaded, and push to main.
  • The road trip page finds it by date and draws it. Delete the day here once it is published, to keep the phone's storage light.

good to know

  • Phone browsers stop GPS the moment the page is in the background, so the page has to stay open and lit. It asks the screen to stay awake while recording — a dash mount and a charging cable are the way.
  • If the page reloads it picks recording back up on its own.
  • Fixes worse than ±100 m are ignored, and days roll over at midnight by themselves.
  • The GPX export is the same route in the format Strava and most map tools import.
