how this works

The phone rides on the dash running a small tracker page that logs a GPS point every hundred metres or so. At the end of a day the tracker exports the route as one small JSON file, which gets dropped into this site's repo and pushed. This page looks for those files and draws whatever it finds, so a day appears here the moment it is published. Built with Claude Code, Leaflet, and OpenStreetMap tiles.
