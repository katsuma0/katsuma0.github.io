styleguide

the design contract for this site. one column, one family, quiet links, section names as small ruled labels. no bars, no tiles, no glass: the page is just the page. light and dark follow the system, and everything prints clean.

rules

  • one stylesheet, assets/site.css. tokens at the top; components below. no frameworks, no build step.
  • type is the system sans stack at 16px/1.65, max measure 42rem. the name is the only big thing on a page.
  • section names are h2: small, uppercase, letterspaced, ruled by a full-width hairline. that is the resume voice; do not reach for bigger headings.
  • links are the one colour on the page. rows of things use .rows/.row; items with a note use .item/.item-note.
  • life entries and sewing projects share the entry voice: a bold title, a dotted leader, the date on the right, photos three across.
  • the header is text: the name on the left, four links on the right. the footer is the contact line and the freshness line. neither is sticky.
  • colours come from the tokens only. light: white paper, near-black ink, one quiet blue. dark: the same page at night. no other hues.
