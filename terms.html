Safety, which matters more than the rest of this page

These apps are references, not safety equipment. Do not let a phone make a decision that your own judgement should make.

  • Do not rely on them in an emergency. Neither app can call for help. Carry a way to reach emergency services where you are going, and tell someone your plan.
  • Identification can be wrong. Never eat a plant, berry or mushroom because an app helped you name it. Many edible species have dangerous look-alikes. If you are not certain from your own knowledge, do not eat it.
  • Wildlife information is general. Bear and wildlife guidance follows the province's published advice, but animals do not read guidance. Keep your distance.
  • Fishing rules change. Seasons, limits and sizes in the app are a convenience copy. The current regulations published by the Government of Ontario are the rule. Check them before you fish.
  • Maps and locations are approximate. Do not navigate by them. Carry a map and a compass, and know how to use them.
